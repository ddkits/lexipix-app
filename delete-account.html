Delete your data

Last updated 2026-09-06

Your data never leaves your device, so you delete it yourself:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data.
  • Or uninstall LexiPix — that removes its storage entirely.
  • If you use device backup (iCloud, Google One), also delete the backup copy in your device settings.

We hold no copy of your content and therefore have nothing to delete on our side.

Purchases are held by Apple and Google under their own policies; deleting your data here does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in your device's subscription settings.
